# Code of conduct

TEA adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) as its code of conduct.

In short: be kind and welcoming, assume good intent, give and take feedback gracefully, and keep discussion about the work. Harassment, insults, personal attacks, and publishing other people's private information are not acceptable anywhere in the project's spaces: issues, pull requests, discussions and chat.

Most TEA users are founders who are new to testing and security. Explain things plainly and never make anyone feel stupid for asking.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour worries you, report it privately to the maintainers through GitHub (the **Security** tab → **Report a vulnerability** works for private reports of any kind). Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove content, or warn, suspend or ban anyone who breaks this code, as described in the Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines.
